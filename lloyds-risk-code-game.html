      Play with the official list

      The built-in deck was compiled from Lloyd's published risk code guidance and mapping notes, with descriptions shortened for play. Lloyd's treats its own risk code spreadsheet as the only authoritative list, and codes change each year of account. Load that spreadsheet here to play with the exact current codes.

      1. Open Lloyd's risk codes page and download the risk codes spreadsheet (Excel).
      2. Choose the file below. It is read in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere.
      3. The game finds the columns headed “Risk code” and “Description”, and uses a class or group column if there is one.

      An unofficial study aid, not affiliated with or endorsed by Lloyd's. Built-in descriptions are paraphrased from Lloyd's risk code guidance and mapping notes (2021 to July 2026 editions) and market bulletins. The 2026 year of account added transactional liability, political risk, credit and financial guarantee codes, and the 2027 year of account adds six more; the built-in deck does not list all of them. Always check the Lloyd's spreadsheet before coding a live risk.
